# Quizontal Cloud — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [Date]

At **Quizontal Cloud** ("we", "us", "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

### 1.1 Information you provide
- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number, company name, and billing address when you register or place an order.
- **Payment information:** bank transfer references and payment receipts you upload for verification.
- **Communications:** support tickets, messages and any information you submit through the client area.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Usage data:** pages viewed, and interactions with our website and client area.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, device information and cookies.

### 1.3 Domain-related information
- For domain registrations, certain information (such as the registrant name, email and, where applicable, organisation) may be required by the relevant domain registry. This information may be published in the public WHOIS database unless WHOIS privacy protection is enabled.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:
1. Provide, operate and maintain our services (domains, hosting, VPS).
2. Process orders, payments and refunds.
3. Manage your account and provide customer support.
4. Verify bank transfer payments.
5. Send transactional and service notifications (invoices, renewals, provisioning updates).
6. Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
7. Improve and secure our services.

## 3. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:
- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the services you have ordered.
- **Legitimate interests** — to improve our services and prevent fraud.
- **Legal obligation** — to comply with applicable laws (for example, tax and accounting requirements).
- **Consent** — where you have given consent for a specific purpose.

## 4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

1. **Service providers** — third parties who help us deliver services, such as domain registries, hosting infrastructure providers (e.g. InterServer), payment processors and email providers.
2. **Domain registries** — information required to register or transfer a domain name.
3. **Legal authorities** — where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We require our service providers to handle your data in accordance with applicable data protection law.
